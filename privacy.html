1. Introduction

EasyRentals Inc ("we," "our," or "us") operates the EasyRentals Accounting integration application ("Software"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Software.

2. Information We Collect

The Software accesses your QuickBooks Online data only with your explicit authorization through OAuth 2.0. We collect the following types of information:

2.1 Accounting Data

  • Company information (name, address, EIN)
  • Chart of Accounts
  • Transactions (invoices, expenses, bills, payments, deposits, checks)
  • Journal entries
  • Account balances

2.2 Payment Data

  • Payment transactions
  • Payment methods

2.3 Authentication Data

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens
  • Company realm ID (QuickBooks company identifier)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide accounting and compliance analysis services
  • Categorize and reclassify transactions
  • Generate compliance reports and tax estimates
  • Identify tax-deductible expenses
  • Flag compliance issues (1099 requirements, personal expenses, etc.)
  • Calculate tax estimates based on C-Corporation requirements

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your system. Specifically:

  • OAuth tokens are stored in tokens.json on your local machine
  • Transaction data is processed in memory and not permanently stored
  • No data is transmitted to our servers or third-party services
  • All processing occurs locally on your computer

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to third parties. Your data remains on your local system and is only accessed through the QuickBooks API with your authorization.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • OAuth 2.0 authentication (industry standard)
  • Secure token storage (local file system)
  • Automatic token refresh to maintain security
  • HTTPS communication with QuickBooks API
  • No data transmission to external servers

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data accessed by the Software
  • Control: Revoke access at any time through QuickBooks Online settings
  • Delete: Remove stored tokens by deleting the tokens.json file
  • Disconnect: Disconnect the Software from QuickBooks at any time

8. Third-Party Services

The Software integrates with:

  • QuickBooks Online (Intuit Inc.): Your use of QuickBooks Online is subject to Intuit's terms of service and privacy policy. We access your QuickBooks data only with your explicit authorization.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

9. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your system:

  • OAuth tokens are retained until you delete tokens.json
  • Transaction data is not permanently stored by the Software
  • You control all data retention through your local file system

10. Children's Privacy

The Software is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

12. Compliance

This application complies with:

  • Industry best practices for data security
